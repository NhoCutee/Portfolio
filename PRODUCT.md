## Users

- **Primary:** Tech Recruiters, Engineering Managers, and Technical Leads evaluating candidates for Software Engineer / Backend & Full Stack roles.
- **Secondary:** Fellow developers, open-source collaborators, and tech community members looking for technical exchange and collaboration.

## Product Purpose

To serve as a high-impact, professional personal portfolio and engineering showcase for Nguyen Huy (@NhoCutee). Success means demonstrating proven engineering competence, robust backend & system architecture expertise, clean full-stack execution, and modern interactive craft—converting visitors into interview invitations and high-value professional opportunities.

## Positioning

A Backend & System-focused Engineer with strong Full Stack execution: deeply proficient in robust backend architectures (Spring Boot, Node.js, PostgreSQL, scalable SaaS, and real-time collaboration) while possessing modern frontend craft (Next.js, TypeScript, Three.js 3D shaders, smooth motion).

- Evaluators scan quickly (15–30 second initial impression): seeking clear role fit, verified experience (VNPT Media, Open Source, FPT University), core tech competencies, live project demos, and frictionless contact / CV download channels.

## Product Principles

1. **Engineering Substance Over Pure Flash:** Visual effects (shaders, animations) should enhance rather than distract from technical credibility and system expertise.
2. **Immediate Scanability:** Technical recruiters must be able to assess core competencies, experience, and contact methods within 15 seconds.
3. **Flawless Craft & Reliability:** Every interaction—from theme toggling to command palette and media embeds—must function without visual glitch or friction.
4. **Authentic Personality:** Professional foundation elevated with tasteful, discovery-oriented details (easter eggs, subtle motion, music) that make the candidate memorable.
